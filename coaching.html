Phase 3 · Implementation
Coaching
Support teachers through implementation with co-teaching, modeling, observation, and feedback.
1
I Know It
  • Review previous visit notes in Service Tracker
  • Review Teacher Dashboard data
  • Use the district-aligned unit map when available — Unit Maps for All Prisms Courses is the general reference
  • Know the coaching-day type and your role within it
2
I Prep For It
  • Confirm the schedule, lesson, and goal for the visit
  • Complete lesson study or preparation for the module
  • Send the teacher a pre-coaching email
  • Confirm materials, rosters, and technology readiness
3
I Move It
  • Support the teacher through co-teaching, modeling, observation, and real-time feedback
  • Protect the instructional purpose and full learning arc
  • Adjust your support based on the coaching-day type and teacher readiness
  • Move the teacher toward greater ownership
Live Coach Tool →
4
I Close the Loop
  • Complete the Service Tracker by class period
  • Complete the Coaching AAR within 48–72 hours
  • Send a warm post-coaching email with a clear strength, next step, and next opportunity
