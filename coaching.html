Phase 3 · Implementation
Coaching
Support teachers through implementation with co-teaching, modeling, observation, and feedback.
1
I Know It
  • Know the teacher's place in the implementation journey — review previous visit notes in Service Tracker and Teacher Dashboard data
  • Know the specific lesson — its Content Module, Skill Builder, Tutorial, Transfer lesson, or CFU, and its purpose and learning goals
  • Use the district-aligned unit map and teacher schedule when available — Unit Maps for All Prisms Courses is the general reference
  • Know the coaching-day type and your role within it
  • Know the five Prisms instructional routines — Hardware & Technology, Just-in-Time Feedback, Writing & Discourse, Assessment & Revision, Spaced Repetition
  • Experience the materials yourself first — annotate the lesson guide, complete the student-facing work, experience the VR module when relevant, and identify the core math/science, likely misconceptions, and logistical challenges
2
I Prep For It
  • Confirm the date, school, teacher, class periods, lesson, and coaching-day type
  • Agree on the goal for the visit and identify 2–3 coaching moves or questions
  • Review the unit map and teacher schedule when applicable, and complete lesson study
  • Confirm materials, rosters, and technology — headsets charged and updated, tech helpers identified for Tutorial Day
  • Send the teacher a pre-coaching email
  • Arrive prepared to support multiple periods whenever the schedule allows
